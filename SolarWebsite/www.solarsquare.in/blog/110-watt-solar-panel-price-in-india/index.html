A 110W solar panel is usually a 12V nominal module, which means that its operating voltage is higher than 12V. As a result, it can trickle charge a 12V battery through a solar charge controller. However, when it comes to the true use of these panels, you must understand that they’re too small to build a kW-scale rooftop solar system that can take care of all your home energy needs.

If used to build a rooftop solar system, there will be many practical challenges. You’ll need tens or hundreds of 110W solar panels to reach the desired system capacity, which, in turn, would require a lot of roof space and significantly increase wiring costs. That’s why higher-wattage solar panels, such as bifacial mono-PERC panels or TOPCon modules, are used to build kW-scale solar systems.

A 110W solar panel is best suited for small backup systems and charging batteries that power devices like LED lights, Wi-Fi routers, and CCTV cameras. It can also be used as a portable solar panel while you go hiking or camping in off-grid locations.

Read on to discover the ins and outs of a 110W solar panel, including its prices, factors that affect its cost, benefits and disadvantages, uses, how it works, its types, and the reason why it’s not a recommended size for building on-grid solar systems for residential and commercial properties.

TL;DR Summary Box: How Much Power Will a 110W Solar Panel Produce?

Every solar panel is tested by its manufacturer under Standard Test Conditions (STC). A 110W solar panel is no different. Under Standard Test Conditions (STC) in a lab, a 110-watt panel is rated to produce 110 watts of DC power. At this maximum output point, it typically generates ~17.5-19 volts, with an open circuit voltage of ~21-22 volts.

However, this does not hold true in the real world, where conditions differ significantly from Standard Test Conditions created in a lab. The actual real-world output is usually 10-25% lower due to heat, dust, wiring losses, and fluctuations in sunlight.

Therefore, on a bright sunny day, a 110W solar panel can produce 80-95 watts of DC power, with an operating voltage of ~16-18.5 volts.

Factors that Affect the 110-watt Solar Panel Price in India

The 110-watt solar panel price in India varies because brands differ in build quality, testing standards, warranty coverage, and after-sales support. Even cell technology plays a major role in cost variation.

Let’s check out all factors that affect the final 110-watt solar panel price in India in 2026:

  • Brand and warranty: Established brands follow stricter quality standards and offer longer warranties. Their panels deliver better durability and stable performance over time. Because of this, branded 110W solar panels cost more than local modules.
  • Type of solar cells used: Monocrystalline cells are more efficient and perform better in limited space compared to polycrystalline cells. Hence, monocrystalline 110W solar panels are costlier than polycrystalline modules of the same rating.
  • Made in India vs imported panels: Imported panels tend to cost more due to import duties, logistics, and distribution margins.
  • GST and shipping inclusion: Some sellers show prices inclusive of GST and shipping, while others add these charges later. Always check what the listed price includes, as this directly affects the final amount you pay.
  • Manufacturer discounts and offers: Seasonal discounts or promotional offers can reduce the final 110-watt solar panel price. The final cost depends on whether you purchase during a sale or at the regular market rate.
  • Seller channel: The 110-watt solar panel price can also vary depending on where you buy the panel. Authorized dealers may charge slightly more but offer genuine products, proper billing, and easier warranty claims. Online local resellers may offer lower prices, but return policies, packaging quality, and after-sales support can differ.

What is a 110W Solar Panel and How Does it Work?

A 110W solar panel is a photovoltaic module rated to produce 110W under Standard Test Conditions (STC) in a lab. In real use, it generates DC power that varies with sunlight intensity and temperature.

Most 110W panels are labeled 12V nominal. This does not mean the panel produces exactly 12 volts. It means the panel is designed to charge a 12V battery using a solar charge controller. The actual operating voltage is usually higher than 12V.

Always remember these key parameters about a 110W solar panel before making a purchase:

  • 110W refers to the peak-rated output under lab conditions.
  • 12V nominal means the operating voltage is above 12V during battery charging.
  • It is commonly used for small off-grid systems and backup setups.
  • You need a charge controller and a battery to store power for night use.

How Does a 110W Solar Panel Work?

A 110-watt solar panel, regardless of its cell type, uses the photovoltaic effect to convert solar energy into DC power, which can be stored in a battery for later use.

Here’s a step-by-step guide on how the panel works:

  • Step 1 – Sunlight hits the solar cells: Sunlight contains tiny energy particles called photons. When these photons strike the silicon solar cells inside the panel, the cells absorb that energy.
  • Step 2 – Electrons get energized: The absorbed energy knocks electrons within the solar cells loose from their atoms. This movement creates free electrons and leaves behind positive holes. Each energized electron creates an electron-hole pair.
  • Step 3 – The internal electric field creates voltage: Inside the solar cell, a built-in electric field at the p-n junction forces electrons and holes to move in opposite directions. This separation creates voltage across the cell.
  • Step 4 – Electrons flow as direct current (DC): When you connect the panel to a circuit, the free electrons move in one direction through the external wires. This one-way flow of electrons is direct current.
  • Step 5 – The charge controller regulates the output: Before charging a battery, the DC power passes through a charge controller. It stabilizes voltage and current, preventing overcharging and protecting connected devices.

What Can a 110W Solar Panel Power?

A 110W solar panel can charge a battery, which you can later use to power devices like phones, flashlights, and laptops while hiking or camping. And this is just one practical use case of these panels. Let’s have a look at all the gadgets a 110-watt solar panel can power.

  • Mobile and gadget charging: It can charge phones, power banks, tablets, Wi-Fi routers, and small electronic devices.
  • LED lighting systems: It can power multiple LED bulbs for several hours in the evening when connected to a battery.
  • Small DC fans: It can run one energy-efficient DC fan for a few hours, especially during sunny days or with adequate battery backup.
  • 12V battery charging: It is commonly used to charge 12V batteries in cabins or rural setups.
  • Camping and RV setups: It supports lights, phone charging, and small fans in camper vans or tents.
  • CCTV and security systems: It can power low-wattage security cameras and DVR systems in remote areas.
  • Small off-grid systems: It works well for sheds, farmhouses, street lights, and small backup systems where power demand is limited.

Do You Need a Battery With a 110W Solar Panel?

If you plan to store solar power for use at night or in emergencies, then yes, a 110W solar panel will need a battery. Since many people use these as portable panels for camping and hiking, a battery makes sense, as stored power is essential for nighttime use in off-grid locations.

However, a 110W solar panel can power gadgets and appliances without a battery during the day. So, whether or not it needs a battery depends on your requirements.

Is a 110-watt Solar Panel Suitable to Build a Rooftop Solar System for Homes?

A 110W solar panel is not practical for building a kilowatt-scale rooftop solar system for homes. Even for a small 3 kW solar system, you would need ~28. Most residential rooftops in India do not have that kind of shadow-free space to accommodate so many panels.

Today, higher-wattage panels like Mono PERC and TOPCon are widely available. These panels offer better efficiency, require fewer modules to reach the same capacity, and reduce overall installation complexity. And because you need fewer panels, mounting structures, wiring, and labor costs are also lower. Therefore, these panels are most recommended for residential and commercial rooftop solar systems.

Here are the main reasons why a 110W panel is not recommended for a kW-scale rooftop system:

  • Too many panels required: A higher system capacity requires combining multiple panels. Using 110W modules requires installing a large number of panels, increasing space requirements and system complexity.
  • Limited rooftop space: Most homes have restricted usable roof area due to water tanks, shading, and structural layout. Small panels make it harder to optimize that space efficiently.
  • Lower efficiency compared to modern panels: Most 110W panels use older cell technologies. Modern high-wattage panels such as TOPCon deliver up to 26% efficiency, producing more power from the same roof area.
  • Higher installation costs: More panels mean more mounting structures, wiring connections, and labor. This increases the total installation cost compared to using fewer high-wattage modules.

What are the Advantages of a 110W Solar Panel?

Compact size is one of the best advantages of a 110W solar panel, as it allows these panels to be easily carried in backpacks while hiking. Plus, when paired with a suitable charge controller and 12V battery, it can provide emergency backup power.

Here are all the main advantages of 110W solar panels that you must know:

  • It’s compact and lightweight: Its smaller dimensions and lighter weight make it easier to mount and carry.
  • Ideal for 12V systems: It pairs well with 12V battery banks commonly used for backup and small off-grid applications.
  • Eco-friendly power source: It generates electricity from solar radiation without producing emissions during operation, helping reduce reliance on grid electricity.
  • Silent operation: It produces solar electricity without noise, making it suitable for homes, RVs, and quiet environments.
  • Suitable for small appliances: It can power LED lights, Wi-Fi routers, CCTV systems, phone charging, and other low-watt devices.

What are the Disadvantages of a 110W Solar Panel?

A 110W solar panel works well for small setups, but it has limitations. Its power output depends heavily on sunlight conditions and is too small for large residential systems.

Here are the main drawbacks:

  • Output drops significantly in weak sunlight: On cloudy days, during winter, or in partial shade, the panel produces less electricity. Without a properly sized battery, your backup time will quickly reduce.
  • Not suitable for kW-scale home systems: A 110W photovoltaic panel cannot be used for kilowatt-scale rooftop installations because you would need too many panels to reach higher capacities.
  • Cannot run heavy appliances: It does not support high-wattage appliances such as air conditioners, refrigerators, water pumps, or devices with high starting surge loads.
  • Requires additional components: You cannot connect appliances or batteries directly to the panel. A charge controller is necessary, which increases the overall cost.
  • Higher cost per watt: Small panels tend to cost more per watt than high-wattage panels used to build rooftop solar systems.
  • Slow charging speed: The panel can charge batteries and small devices, but it does so gradually. It cannot match the fast charging speed of high-wattage solar systems.

FAQs

How many amps does a 110W solar panel produce?

A 110W solar panel can produce ~6 amps under ideal sunlight. Using the formula Amps = Watts ÷ Volts, at an operating voltage of 18V, it generates about 6.1 amps. When charging a 12V battery at 14V, it may reach 7 to 8 amps. Actual output varies with sunlight and temperature conditions.

Why does the 110W solar panel price vary between brands?

The price varies because brands differ in build quality, cell technology, efficiency, warranty coverage, and after-sales support. Some panels use better glass, stronger frames, and higher-grade solar cells, which improve durability and performance but also raise costs.

Is a 110W solar panel enough to run a home?

No, a 110W solar panel is not enough to run a full home. It is suitable only for small loads, such as LED lights, a Wi-Fi router, phone charging, or a small DC fan. Running major appliances such as refrigerators, air conditioners, or water pumps requires a much larger solar system.

Can a 110W solar panel charge a 12V battery?

Yes, a 110W solar panel can charge a 12V battery when connected through a proper charge controller. Most 110W panels are designed as 12V nominal panels. Hence, their operating voltage is suitable for safely charging 12V battery systems.

Can I connect two 110-watt panels to one 12V battery?

Yes, you can connect two 110W panels to one 12V battery, but you must use a suitable charge controller for that.

  • For most 12V systems, panels are connected in parallel. This keeps the voltage constant while increasing current.
  • Connecting panels in series increases voltage. This can work with an MPPT charge controller, but only if the controller’s maximum PV input voltage is not exceeded.

Always check the charge controller’s voltage and current limits before connecting multiple panels.
