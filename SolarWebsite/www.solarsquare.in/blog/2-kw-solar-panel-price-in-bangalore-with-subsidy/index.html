How to Apply for a Solar Subsidy in Bangalore?

You can register your account on the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal to receive a subsidy straight into your bank account.

Here’s a walkthrough of the simple steps you can follow to get your claim approved:

  • Step 1 – Visit the official portal: Head to the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal. Start by clicking the Apply for Rooftop Solar button on the homepage.
  • Step 2 – Create your profile: Select your state, district, and electricity board (DISCOM). Then, enter your Customer Account Number. This will link your solar application to your active power connection.
  • Step 3 – Complete the online form: Enter your name, contact details, and upload a copy of your latest electricity bill.
  • Step 4 – Submit your bank details: Fill in your bank account information and upload a cancelled cheque. This is where your subsidy amount will be credited once your system is approved.
  • Step 5 – Choose an authorized solar vendor: Pick a DISCOM-approved installer such as SolarSquare for the best solar panel installation in Bangalore.
  • Step 6 – Finalize and upload the agreement: Review the terms with your chosen installer, sign the contract, and upload a scanned copy to the portal.
  • Step 7 – Get your system installed: After installation, the DISCOM will visit the site and set up a bi-directional net meter or smart meter.
  • Step 8 – Commissioning and verification: Your local DISCOM will inspect the system and issue a Solar Commissioning Certificate to confirm it’s working as expected.
  • Step 9 – File your subsidy claim: Upload the commissioning certificate to the same portal and submit your final request for subsidy disbursement.
  • Step 10 – Subsidy is sent directly to your bank: Once approved, the subsidy is transferred to your account, usually within 30 working days.

Whenever needed, electricity can be drawn directly from the grid.

However, if you live in areas where grid supply is unavailable or unreliable, battery-based off-grid and hybrid solar systems will be better investments.

Which Type of Solar Panel is Best for Homes in Bangalore?

The two most recommended solar panels for residential and commercial installations are TOPCon solar panels and mono-PERC solar panels made of half-cut silicon solar cells.

Here’s why these high-efficiency solar panels are preferred:

  • They generate more power: Since their efficiency rating is higher, they generate more power. The efficiency is 25-26% for TOPCon panels and up to 22.5% for mono-PERC solar panels.
  • They perform better even under partial shadows: Since they have a half-cut cell design, if shadows cover one half of the panel, the other half continues to produce electricity.
  • They have a low temperature coefficient: These panels perform better even as the temperature climbs because of their lower temperature coefficient.

All the factors listed above eventually translate to higher solar savings when compared to low-efficiency solar panels, such as polycrystalline panels.

Which Factors Affect the Solar Panel Price for Homes in Bangalore?

Multiple factors that affect the final solar panel price for homes in Bangalore include DISCOM charges, types of solar inverter and panels, type and capacity of solar system, height of mounting structures, and annual maintenance contract (AMC).

Let’s understand how the major factors affect the final estimated solar rooftop cost in Bangalore in detail:

  • Panel quality and wattage: High-efficiency panels, such as 540Wp bifacial ones, cost more upfront, but they generate more electricity per square foot, resulting in better long-term savings.
  • Inverter type and brand: A microinverter costs up to 90% more than a string inverter. It should be chosen only if a rooftop is afflicted with shadows. Otherwise, a string inverter is the best option.
  • Mounting structure height: The height and material of your solar mounting structure can change the final solar panel installation cost in Bangalore. Taller structures need more steel, more labor, and extra reinforcement.
  • Type of solar system: On-grid systems are the most affordable. Hybrid and off-grid systems require lithium batteries, which add significantly to the total cost.
  • System size (kW rating): Bigger systems cost more overall, but the price per kW often goes down as size increases. The right size depends on your electricity usage.
  • Subsidy eligibility: If your home qualifies for the central government’s subsidy, you’ll get a direct discount that reduces the amount you pay out of pocket.
  • After-sales service plan (AMC): Opting for a full annual maintenance contract (AMC) adds to the upfront cost, but helps keep your system in top condition. A well-maintained system generates power more efficiently, resulting in higher savings in the long run.
  • DISCOM-specific charges: Application, inspection, and solar net metering fees vary slightly across different DISCOMs. These small charges add up depending on your state and city.
  • Roof condition and design: Sloping rooftops, tiled surfaces, weak RCC, or shadow-prone areas may need special mounting solutions or civil work, which increases cost.
  • Price of raw materials: Market rates for solar components, such as steel, glass, silicon, and copper, fluctuate with global supply and currency fluctuations, affecting pricing.

FAQs

Q1. Can I run an AC on a 2 kW solar system?

Ans. A 2 kW solar system is not an ideal size to run an air conditioner. For one single 1.5-ton AC, the ideal system size is 3 kW. For two 1.5-ton ACs, the ideal system size is 10 kW.

Q2. What size roof do I need for a 2 kW solar system in Bangalore?

Ans. A 2 kW solar system can be installed in 200 square feet of shadow-free area on the roof.

Q3. How many solar panels are needed for a 2 kW solar system in Bangalore?

Ans. Here’s a simple formula you can use to calculate the number of solar panels based on the module capacity you choose: Number of solar panels = System capacity (in watts) ÷ the module capacity (in watts) Based on the formula above, you will require ~4 solar panels of 540 watts to build a 2 kW solar system. IMPORTANT: The number of panels shared here is an estimate only. The final count can be higher or lower, depending on the panel wattage, the layout your roof allows, shading, and the exact system size you ultimately choose. Since solar panels come in whole units, the installed capacity may end up slightly above or below the stated kW rating. This can also change the system's total cost, as using more panels increases hardware and installation expenses.
