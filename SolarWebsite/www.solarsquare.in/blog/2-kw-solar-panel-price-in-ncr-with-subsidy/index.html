How to Apply for a Solar Subsidy in NCR?

Before you learn the basic steps to apply for a solar subsidy in NCR, let’s give you a glimpse of some important eligibility conditions that must be met to be considered eligible to receive the PM Surya Ghar subsidy.

  • You must be an Indian citizen installing a grid-connected solar system.
  • You are installing solar for residential use only. Commercial rooftop systems and off-grid solar systems are not covered under this scheme.
  • You are using made-in-India solar panels.
  • You legally own the rooftop where the installation will occur.
  • You have an active electricity connection in your name with your city’s DISCOM.

If you’re eligible to receive a solar subsidy in NCR, you can follow the simple steps listed below:

Step 1 – Start with the official portal: Go to the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal and hit the “Apply for Rooftop Solar” button.

Step 2 – Register using your DISCOM details: Choose your state and electricity board. Then, enter your customer account number from your latest bill.

Step 3 – Fill in the rooftop solar application form on the portal: Add your name, address, and basic contact details. Also, upload a soft copy of your most recent electricity bill.

Step 4 – Enter your bank details: Input your bank account information and upload a cancelled cheque to the portal.

Step 5 – Pick a registered solar installer: Select a company, such as SolarSquare, from the list of approved vendors on your DISCOM’s portal.

Step 6 – Sign and upload the agreement: Once you agree to the installer’s terms, sign the contract and upload it back to the portal.

Step 7 – Net meter is installed post-installation: After your system is up and running, the DISCOM installs a bi-directional meter or a smart meter to track energy export and import.

Step 8 – You will receive a commissioning certificate: Your DISCOM inspects the system and issues a Solar Commissioning Certificate if everything complies with the rules.

Step 9 – Claim your subsidy: Log back into the portal, upload the solar commissioning certificate, and submit your subsidy claim for disbursement.

Step 10 – Get the money in your account: Once your claim is approved, the subsidy amount is transferred straight to your bank within 30 days.

Top Factors that Affect the 2 kW Solar Panel Price in India

Major factors that influence the 2 kW solar panel price in India include your DISCOM fees, types of solar inverters and panels you choose, type of solar system, your subsidy eligibility, and even the condition and height of the roof.

Let’s understand all factors that influence the 2 kW solar panel price in India in detail:

  • DISCOM application fees: Each state electricity board sets its own charges for approvals, site inspections, and net meter installation. These vary across cities and will reflect in your total solar system installation cost.
  • Type of solar panels you choose: Monocrystalline panels with half-cut cell technology, like 540 Wp bifacial solar panels, cost more than polycrystalline ones, which are now almost obsolete. However, high-efficiency panels generate more power per square foot, resulting in higher solar savings.
  • Inverter selection: Microinverters can be 90% more expensive than string inverters. It’s better to go with string inverters unless too many trees shade your rooftop during peak sunshine hours.
  • Mounting structure complexity: Installing panels on high or sloped roofs, or on buildings surrounded by shade, may require taller, reinforced structures, which adds to both material and labour costs.
  • On-grid, hybrid, or off-grid system: On-grid systems are the most affordable. Hybrid and off-grid solar systems require lithium batteries, which significantly increase their cost.
  • Annual maintenance contract (AMC): AMCs aren’t mandatory, but getting one means fewer headaches. Plans that include yearly checkups, panel cleaning, and inverter maintenance add to upfront cost but reduce future repair expenses. It proves to be much more cost-effective in the long run.
  • Subsidy eligibility: If your home qualifies for the central subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Yojana, your final price drops significantly.
  • Roof type and design constraints: Tiled, sloped, or weak roofs might need custom mounting or reinforcement. This increases complexity and raises the cost of solar installation.
  • City-level cost differences: Prices for transport, labour, and permissions vary from city to city. Solar in metro areas may be costlier than in Tier-2 cities.

FAQs

Q1. Can I run AC on a 2 kW solar system?

Ans. Practically, you can run any appliance on a 2 kW solar system. However, 2 kW is not considered an ideal size for an air conditioner because it consumes a lot of electricity. If you use a 2 kW system to power an AC, you'll rely more on grid electricity, defeating the purpose of getting zero bills by going solar. If you have a 1.5-ton AC at your home, 3 kW will be the ideal system size for you.

Q2. How many solar panels are required for a 2 kW solar system?

Ans. If you use 540Wp high-efficiency solar panels, it will require ~4 panels to build a 2 kW solar system. So, the number of panels needed is directly related to the module capacity. You can use the following formula to find the number of solar panels needed to reach the desired system capacity: Number of solar panels needed = Solar panel’s capacity in watts ÷ solar system’s capacity in watts.

Q3. What size roof do I need for 2 kW?

Ans. A 2 kW on-grid solar system would require 200 square feet of shadow-free area on the roof for proper installation.

Q4. Is 2 kW enough to run a house in NCR?

Ans. A 2 kW solar system is enough for a 1-2 BHK home with yearly electricity consumption of 2,800-2,900 units. If your annual consumption falls in the said range and you do not have an air conditioner, a 2 kW solar system will be able to power all common home appliances, including 2-3 ceiling fans, 1-2 LED bulbs, 1-2 tube lights, 1 refrigerator, 1 LED TV, 1 washing machine, a microwave, an induction cooktop, and a mixer/grinder.
