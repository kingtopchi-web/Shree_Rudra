The subsidy homeowners receive in Telangana is offered by the central government, not the state government, under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana. The maximum solar panel subsidy in Telangana under this scheme is capped at ₹ 78,000 for solar energy systems with a capacity of 3 kW or higher.

  • Solar subsidy in Telangana for 1-2 kW solar systems: ₹ 30,000 per kilowatt.

How to Apply for a Solar Panel Subsidy in Telangana?

You can head to the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal and follow the steps listed below to file your subsidy claim.

Step 1 – Visit the official portal: Go to the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal and click Apply for Rooftop Solar to start the registration process.

Step 2 – Register as a consumer: Select your state, district, and DISCOM. Then, enter your customer account number to create a profile.

Step 3 – Complete the application form: Fill in your personal details and upload a copy of your latest electricity bill.

Step 4 – Provide bank details: Enter your bank account information and upload a cancelled cheque, so the subsidy can be transferred directly to this registered bank account later.

Step 5 – Choose an empanelled installer: Pick a company, such as SolarSquare, approved by your DISCOM to carry out rooftop solar installation in your city.

Step 6 – Submit the signed agreement: After signing the vendor’s agreement, upload the signed copy to the portal.

Step 7 – DISCOM installs the net meter: Once your installation company installs the rooftop solar system, your DISCOM sets up a bi‑directional meter or a smart meter to measure both export and import of power.

Step 8 – Get your commissioning certificate: The DISCOM inspects the system and, if everything is correct, issues a commissioning certificate.

Step 9 – Claim your subsidy online: Log back into the portal and upload the commissioning certificate to file your subsidy claim.

Step 10 – Subsidy credited to your account: After approval, the subsidy is directly transferred to your bank account within 30 days.

Who Is Eligible for the Surya Ghar Yojana Solar Panel Subsidy in Telangana?

You must meet the eligibility criteria listed below to be considered eligible for receiving a subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana:

  • You’re an Indian citizen installing a grid-connected solar system.
  • You have a valid electricity connection in your name.You are installing solar for residential usage only. Commercial rooftop systems are not applicable for subsidy under this scheme.
  • You’ve chosen made-in-India DCR solar panels.
  • You legally own the rooftop where installation will occur.
  • You have never previously received a subsidy for systems up to 3 kW.

What Are the Top Factors That Affect the 2 kW Rooftop Solar System Cost?

The cost of installing a 2 kW solar system varies from city to city in Telangana. Multiple factors, such as DISCOM charges, mounting structure height, and types of solar inverters and solar panels, are responsible for this cost variation.

Let’s explore all the factors responsible for the 2 kW rooftop solar system cost variation in detail:

  • DISCOM application and net metering charges: Each power utility (DISCOM) has its own fee structure for processing applications, inspecting sites, and setting up net meters. These charges differ across regions and are factored into your final solar quote.
  • Solar panel efficiency and technology: Modules with higher efficiency ratings, such as TOPCon half-cut solar panels, can be more expensive per watt. But because they produce more energy in less space, they deliver stronger returns over time. In fact, TOPCon modules are currently the best solar panels on the market because they perform well in shade and deliver constant output even as temperatures rise.
  • Type of solar inverter: While microinverters offer panel-level control, they are also 90% costlier than string inverters. Choose microinverters only if your rooftop is affected by extreme shadows during peak sunshine hours.
  • Height and complexity of the mounting structure: Elevated structures on shaded rooftops require stronger frames, additional materials, and more labour. This can increase the overall solar installation cost.
  • Maintenance plan selection (AMC): Opting for an annual maintenance contract (AMC) can add to your upfront cost, but covers essentials like solar panel cleaning, inverter checks, and system monitoring. An AMC ends up saving more money in the long run.
  • System configuration (on-grid, off-grid, or hybrid): On-grid systems are the most cost-effective. Adding lithium batteries for backup significantly increases the cost and is not really necessary in cities with a reliable grid connection.
  • Central subsidy eligibility: If your solar system qualifies under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, you’ll receive a direct subsidy that lowers the final installation cost.
  • City or installation location: Prices vary based on where you live. Labour charges, transport logistics, and local vendor pricing can make the same system slightly more or less expensive in different cities across Telangana.
  • Roof design, slope, and shadow impact: Flat RCC roofs are easiest to work with. Sloped, tiled, or shaded rooftops may need custom mounting or load analysis, which increases the overall cost.

FAQs

Q1. How many ACs can run on a 2 kW solar system?

Ans. A 2 kW solar system is not the ideal size to power an air conditioner. For running a 1.5-ton single AC, you need a 3 kW solar system.

Q2. What is the maximum subsidy on solar panels in India?

Ans. The maximum subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is capped at ₹ 78,000 for systems equal to or greater than 3 kW.

Q3. How long will a 2 kW solar system last?

Ans. A well-maintained solar system whose panels are cleaned periodically by professionals continues generating electricity for 25 years. Even after 25 years, it can operate at 80% efficiency.

Q4. Do solar panels work at night?

Ans. No, solar panels do not generate electricity at night since there’s no sunlight at night. Instead, your on-grid solar system will import electricity from the grid at night to keep the load running.
