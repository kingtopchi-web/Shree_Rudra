How to Apply for a Solar Subsidy in Lucknow?

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal serves as the single point of entry for the subsidy process. When you submit your rooftop solar application on the portal, the details you enter are used in two ways. They are checked for the central subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and also shared with the Uttar Pradesh New and Renewable Energy Development Agency (UPNEDA) for the state-level subsidy.

You can follow these steps to complete the subsidy process in Lucknow:

  • Step 1 – Open the official portal: Go to the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal and click the Apply for Rooftop Solar option.
  • Step 2 – Create your consumer profile on the portal: Choose your state, your district, and your DISCOM. Then enter your Customer Account Number to create your profile.
  • Step 3 – Fill out the rooftop solar form: Enter your personal details and enter the figures from your most recent electricity bill.
  • Step 4 – Add banking information: Upload the documents requested by the portal, provide your bank account details, and upload a cancelled cheque for verification.
  • Step 5 – Select an empanelled installer: Pick an installation company from the list approved by your DISCOM for rooftop solar projects.
  • Step 6 – Upload the signed agreement: Sign the agreement you receive from your chosen installer, then upload the signed copy to the National Portal.
  • Step 7 – Net meter is installed: After the solar system is installed, your DISCOM fits a bi-directional or smart meter that records both the electricity you draw from the grid and the electricity you send back.
  • Step 8 – Get the commissioning certificate: Once the solar plant has been checked and confirmed operational, your DISCOM issues the Solar Commissioning Certificate.
  • Step 9 – File your subsidy claim: Log in to the National Portal and submit the Commissioning Certificate to process your subsidy claim.
  • Step 10 – Receive the subsidy: After your claim is approved, the sanctioned subsidy amount is credited directly to your bank account within 30 days.

IMPORTANT: Because UPNEDA reads your information directly from the portal, you are not asked to fill out a separate state subsidy form. Provide correct information and upload the right documents. After your system is installed and the net meter is in place, UPNEDA reviews the data on the portal and confirms that the installation matches what was applied for. Upon successful verification, the state subsidy is approved and transferred directly to your bank account, along with the PM Surya Ghar subsidy.

Top Factors that Affect the Cost of a 3 kW Solar System in Lucknow

The cost of a 3 kW solar system in Lucknow can vary based on the type of solar system, panel, and inverter you choose. It will also be influenced by the DISCOM’s metering charges, the AMC contract you choose, and the height of the solar mounting structure.

Let’s check out how each of these factors impacts the final cost of a 3 kW solar panel system in Lucknow:

  • The type of solar system you choose: On-grid systems remain the most affordable because they do not need battery storage. Off-grid and hybrid systems include batteries, so you can run appliances during power cuts, which naturally increases the total price. The choice will ultimately boil down to how reliable your grid supply is.
  • The type of solar panel: Higher-efficiency panels cost more. Half-cut TOPCon panels cost more than older polycrystalline models, but they generate more electricity and require fewer units to reach the same capacity.
  • Solar inverter type: String inverters are far more economical than microinverters. Microinverters make sense only when the roof has shading issues during peak sunshine hours.
  • Height of the solar mounting structure: Taller structures need more steel, more labor, and more fabrication time. Each of these adds to the installation cost.
  • DISCOM application and net metering charges: Your electricity provider sets its own fees for reviewing applications, inspecting the system, and installing the net meter. These charges differ from one DISCOM to another, even within Lucknow.
  • Annual maintenance plan selection (AMC): Signing up for an annual maintenance contract increases the upfront cost slightly, but it covers panel cleaning, general check-ups, and inverter servicing. This reduces long-term performance losses and helps prevent avoidable breakdowns, saving money in the long run.
  • Roof design, slope, and shadow impact: Flat concrete roofs cost the least to install on. Tiled, sloped, or shaded rooftops often require custom mounting or additional structural assessment, which increases the final 3 kW solar system price in Lucknow.

FAQs

Q1. How many ACs can run on a 3 kW solar system in Lucknow?

Ans. A 3 kW solar system that generates 4,200 to 4,350 units of electricity in a year can power a single 1.5-ton air conditioner.

Q2. What is the lifespan of a 3 kW solar panel system?

Ans. A professionally cleaned and maintained 3 kW solar system can last for 25 years, and continue working at 80% efficiency after that.

Q3. How many units will a 3 kW solar panel produce?

Ans. With an annual generation of about 4,200 to 4,350 units, a 3 kW solar system delivers ~11.5 to ~12 units a day, when averaged across the year. The day-to-day output, though, does not stay the same. Summer brings the strongest production, while monsoon months pull the numbers down. Over the full year, the variations balance out because the dip during rainy days is offset by the higher yield during the hotter months.

Q4. What size roof is needed for a 3 kW solar system?

Ans. You will require approximately 300 square feet of shadow-free area on your roof for a 3 kW on-grid solar system.
