What’s the 30 kW Solar System Subsidy in India?

The Indian government offers group housing societies (GHS) installing on-grid rooftop solar systems a subsidy of Rs. 18,000 per kW up to 500 kW (@3 kW per house) for common facilities, including EV charging. 

This subsidy is available under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and can be availed under the following conditions only:

  • The electricity meter is in the name of the housing society, not the builder.
  • The modules being installed are DCR made-in-India solar panels.

If you’re installing a 30 kW solar system, you cannot claim a solar subsidy under the following conditions:

  • You’re installing a 30 kW ground-mounted solar system.
  • You’re installing a 30 kW off-grid solar system.
  • You’re installing a 30 kW on-grid rooftop solar system for commercial purposes.

Simply put, you will get a subsidy for 30 kW PV systems under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana only if you’re a housing society installing an on-grid rooftop solar panel system.

How to Apply for a 30 kW Solar System Subsidy in India?

You can apply for a subsidy on the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal by following a series of simple steps:

  • Step 1 – Access the national portal: Visit the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Portal and click ‘Apply for Rooftop Solar’.
  • Step 2 – Register your account: Select your state, district, and DISCOM. Then, enter your ‘Customer Account Number’.
  • Step 3 – Complete installation form: Provide personal details (like your name and gender) and your latest electricity bill information.
  • Step 4 – Upload required documents: Submit Proof of Identity, Proof of Ownership, and your most recent electricity bill.
  • Step 5 – Submit bank details: After uploading the documents, enter your bank account information and upload a cancelled cheque on the portal.
  • Step 6 – Choose an approved vendor: Select a DISCOM‑approved solar installer like SolarSquare to install rooftop solar at your housing society.
  • Step 7 – Net meter installation: Once installation is complete, your DISCOM will fit a bi‑directional net‑meter to track imported and exported units.
  • Step 8 – Obtain commissioning certificate: Your DISCOM issues a Solar Commissioning Certificate confirming the system is live.
  • Step 9 – Apply for subsidy: Log back into your customer account on the portal and submit your Commissioning Certificate to claim the subsidy. 
  • Step 10 – Receive subsidy: The approved subsidy amount is transferred to your bank account within 60 days of claim approval.

Factors Affecting the 30 kW Solar Panel System Price in India in 2025

The 30 kW rooftop solar system price in India varies based on multiple factors, including the type of solar system you’re installing (on-grid, off-grid, or hybrid), the type and efficiency of solar panels and solar inverters, and the number of floors in your housing society. 

Let’s check out all the factors that influence the final 30 kW solar panel system price in India in 2025:

  • Type of solar system: On-grid solar systems are the cheapest and easiest to maintain. Since off-grid and hybrid solar systems have lithium batteries, they’re more expensive to install and maintain.
  • Whether or not you’re eligible for a subsidy: The government offers housing societies a subsidy for installing on-grid rooftop solar systems. If you’re not eligible for this subsidy, your system will cost more. 
  • Type of solar panels: Made-in-India DCR modules make you eligible for a subsidy, reducing the cost of a 30 kW solar system. Additionally, higher-efficiency solar panels tend to be more expensive than low-efficiency modules, as they generate more electricity and perform better.
  • Type of solar inverter: An MPPT solar inverter is expensive, but it also ensures your solar panels are producing the maximum possible output at all times during any given point in the day.
  • DISCOM charges: DISCOMs may charge additional fees based on grid connectivity upgrades, transformer requirements, or safety inspections.
  • Mounting structure height: The taller the mounting structure, the higher the cost of materials and labor.
  • Type of after-sales service: Opting for a full-service Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) or extended warranty can increase your upfront cost but reduce long-term maintenance expenses.
  • Roof height: Higher rooftops may require more scaffolding, labor, and safety compliance measures during installation, all of which can raise overall costs.
  • Number of floors in your society: Societies with multiple floors need longer cable runs and complex routing to reach the meter room. It increases equipment and labor charges, affecting the final installation cost.

What Are the Types of a 30 kW Solar System in India?

The three main types of a 30 kW solar system in India are on-grid, off-grid, and hybrid. An on-grid solar system is connected to the main utility grid. An off-grid solar system is connected to a lithium battery bank, not the utility grid. And, a hybrid solar system is connected to both the grid and a lithium battery bank.

For housing societies with a reliable grid, a 30 kW on-grid solar system is the best option. It has a quick payback period of ~1.5 years, and since it doesn’t require a battery, its installation cost is significantly lower than that of the other two types. 

Let’s check out all types of 30 kW solar systems in detail.

#1. 30 kW On-Grid Solar System and How it Works

A 30 kW on-grid solar system is connected to the main utility grid. When the solar panels in the solar system generate more power than can be consumed in real time, the bi-directional net meter exports it to the grid. At night, the meter imports electricity from the grid to keep loads running. 

Here are the main components of an on-grid solar system and how they work:

  • Solar panels generate DC power: Solar panels are composed of solar photovoltaic cells that capture visible light from solar radiation and convert it into direct current (DC).
  • Solar panels are secured on top of solar mounting structures: Elevated mounting structures keep solar panels secured. The high-quality WindPro Mount™ IIT-Bombay-approved mounting structures by SolarSquare are perfect for high-rise societies since they can withstand cyclonic winds up to 170 kmph. 
  • DC output from solar panels goes to a solar inverter: The solar inverter converts direct current into alternating current (AC).
  • Additional units are sent to the grid during daytime: If the solar system produces more power than can be consumed, the bi-directional net meter sends it to the grid.
  • Electricity is imported from the grid at night: The net meter imports electricity from the grid to keep the loads running at night.
  • The net meter records the unit exchange between the grid and the solar system: This is very important. Ultimately, it determines whether your system supplied more units to the grid or consumed more electricity from the grid. You get a solar bill only if you imported more units than your system exported. 

#2. 30 kW Off-Grid Solar System and How it Works

An off-grid solar system, also known as a standalone solar system, isn’t connected to the utility grid. Instead, it’s connected to a battery bank that stores solar energy.  

Here are the main components of a 30 kW off-grid solar system and how they work:

  • Solar panels capture sunlight: These panels, just like the ones in on-grid systems, capture sunlight and convert it to DC electricity. 
  • Solar inverter uses its inbuilt MPPT solar charge controller to charge batteries: The charge controller ensures batteries are charged safely.
  • Batteries supply electricity at night, or whenever needed: The inverter converts DC from batteries to AC to keep the load running at night.

#3. 30 kW Hybrid Solar System and How it Works

A 30 kW hybrid solar system is the most expensive type as it’s connected to both the grid and a lithium battery bank, making the setup complicated. 

Here are the main components and their role:

  • Solar panels generate electricity as usual: They capture sunlight and turn it into DC power.
  • Solar inverters convert a portion of DC to AC: During the daytime, the load continues to run on the electricity produced by the panels. The inverter turns DC to AC for this purpose.
  • Additional units are sent to the built-in charge controller: The inverter checks the battery status and, if needed, uses additional generated current to charge the batteries safely.
  • If anything remains, it is transferred to the grid: The bi-directional net meter sends any excess electricity remaining to the utility grid.
  • Energy stored in the batteries is used at night or whenever needed: The inverter converts DC to AC, keeping the load running whenever needed.

On-Grid vs Off-Grid vs Hybrid: Which is Better and Why?

An on-grid solar system is better than off-grid and hybrid solar panel systems because its payback period is ~1.5 years, and it’s the most affordable option. Moreover, there’s no limit to sending additional electricity generated by the solar panels to the utility grid. 

Since net metering allows unlimited unit exchange between the grid and the solar system, housing societies with a reliable grid connection don’t need to invest in batteries, which are not a one-time expense. Lithium batteries are expensive to install, maintain, as well as replace.

It’s better to install rooftop solar systems because ground-mounted solar systems are expensive, require substantial investment to purchase land for the solar system setup, and the government doesn’t provide any subsidy assistance for these solar systems.

Q1. How much does a 30 kW solar system cost in India in 2025?

Ans. An on-grid 30 kW rooftop solar system price in India in 2025 ranges from ~Rs. 40,000* to 45,000* per kW + 13.8% GST for DCR projects after you avail of a subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana. *Please note: The above-mentioned 30 kW solar system price list is indicative as of 27th August 2025 for the SolarSquare’s Blue 4-8ft variant. Q2. What’s the payback period for a 30 kW solar panel system?

Ans. The payback period for on-grid 30 kW solar panel systems in India after a subsidy is ~1.5 years. It means that the initial investment cost is recovered within 1.5 years.

Q3. How much space is needed for a 30 kW solar panel system?

Ans. It requires ~3,000 square feet of shadow-free area on the roof to install a 30 kW on-grid solar system comfortably.

Q4. How many units can a 30 kW solar system produce?

Ans. A 30 kW solar panel system can produce 40,500* to 42,000* units of electricity annually. *Please note: The number of units generated by a 30 kW solar panel system listed above are indicative. The actual units generated can vary based on the pollution level in your city, peak sunshine hours your roof receives, shadows from surrounding objects, temperature and weather conditions in your city, tilt angle and efficiency of the solar panels used, inverter technology used, etc.
