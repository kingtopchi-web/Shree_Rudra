What is the 4 kW Solar Panel Price in India With and Without A Subsidy?

The central government offers homeowners a subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana. Here are the subsidy rates:

  • Solar subsidy for 1-2 kW solar systems: ₹ 30,000 per kilowatt
  • Solar subsidy for additional capacity over and above 2 kW up to 3 kW: ₹ 18,000
  • Solar subsidy for 3 kW or higher capacity: ₹ 78,000

However, if you’re from UP and Delhi, the state government offers an additional subsidy on top of the subsidy offered by the center for installing a rooftop solar panel system for homes.

Here are the additional state subsidy details for UP residents:

  • State subsidy for 1-2 kW solar systems: ₹ 15,000 / kW
  • State subsidy for systems >=2 kW: ₹ 30,000

Here are the additional state subsidy details for Delhi residents:

  • State subsidy for 1-2 kW solar systems: ₹ 26,000 / kW
  • State subsidy for 2-3 kW solar systems: ₹ 10,000/ kW
  • State subsidy for systems >=3 kW: ₹ 78,000

Now that you’ve seen the prices of all three types, let’s help you understand which type is best for residential installations and why.

On-Grid Solar System

An on-grid rooftop solar system is also known as a grid-tied PV system. This system is connected to the main utility grid through a bi-directional net meter that keeps a record of the unit exchange between your solar system and the utility grid:

  • If your system exports more electricity to the grid than it imports, you earn credits from the DISCOM.
  • If your system imports more units from the grid than it exports, you pay for the difference.

If the utility grid in your area is reliable and does not experience frequent, prolonged power outages since the government offers a subsidy for installing on-grid rooftop solar systems.

Off-Grid Solar System

Unlike on-grid systems, which are connected to the main utility grid, off-grid systems are standalone (not attached to the power grid). Instead, they have a battery bank (usually composed of lithium batteries) which keeps homes powered during the nighttime.

Off-grid systems are suitable for remote areas with no power grid. Here’s why you should go for on-grid systems instead of off-grid systems if your grid is reliable:

  • No subsidy assistance: The Indian government does not offer a subsidy for installing off-grid systems.
  • More expensive: Off-grid systems rely on batteries, which are not only expensive to purchase but also expensive to maintain.

Hybrid Solar System

A hybrid solar system is connected to the utility grid and the battery bank, both. It’s also the most expensive of the three types. An on-grid system, which is a lot cheaper than the hybrid system and much easier to maintain since it doesn’t require batteries, still emerges as a clear victor as long as the power grid is reliable.

Is 4 kW the Right Capacity For Your Home?

A 4 kW solar system can generate 5,600 to 5,800 units of electricity yearly. If the yearly power consumption in your home falls within this unit bracket, installing a 4 kW solar system will take care of all your energy requirements.

Here’s a list of appliances that a 4 kW solar system can easily support:

  • AC
  • Washing machine
  • Refrigerator
  • TV
  • Home lighting system and fans
  • Geyser
  • All common kitchen appliances

Not sure how many units of electricity your household consumes? Here’s what you can do:

  • Review your electricity bills from last year
  • Calculate the average number of units consumed in a year
  • Divide the yearly units consumed at your home by the number of units a 1 kW solar system generates (1,400* units/year).

*On average, a 1 kW solar system produces 1,400-1,450 units per year.

Q2. How many solar panels are required for a 4 kW solar system?

Ans. Here’s the formula to calculate the number of panels: System capacity (in watts) divided by the module capacity (in watts) Suppose, you’re using 610-watt modules to erect a 4 kW system, here’s the calculation for the number of panels: 4,000 watts divided by 610 watts = 6.55, rounded off to 7 panels.

Q3. What is the subsidy for a 4 kW solar system?

Ans. Here are the subsidy details for a 4 kW on-grid rooftop solar system in India: You will get a subsidy of ₹ 78,000 for installing a 4 kW solar system under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana. If you’re from UP and Delhi, you will get an additional state subsidy of ₹ 30,000 and ₹ 78,000, bringing the total subsidy on an on-grid 4 kW solar system to ₹ 108,000 and ₹ 156,000.

Q4. How many ACs can run on a 4 kW solar system?

Ans. A 4 kW solar system can run one or two ACs (1.5 tons each). Whether it supports one AC or two depends on how many other heavy appliances you are using.

Q5. Is 4 kW enough to run a house?

Ans. Yes. A 4 kW solar system generates 5,600 to 5,800 units of electricity every year which is enough to run a 3 BHK home.
