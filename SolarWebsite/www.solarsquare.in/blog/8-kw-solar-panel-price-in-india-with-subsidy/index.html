How to Apply for a Solar Subsidy in India?

You can apply for a solar subsidy in India through the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal if you meet the below-listed eligibility criteria:

  • You have never received a solar subsidy for systems up to 3 kW before.
  • You are installing ALMM-approved, made-in-India panels.
  • You’re installing a grid-connected solar system because off-grid and commercial rooftop systems are not eligible under this subsidy scheme. 
  • You legally own the property where you’re installing the solar system.
  • You have an active electricity connection in your name with your DISCOM.

If you fulfill these basic eligibility conditions, follow the steps listed below to claim your solar subsidy in India:

  • Step 1 – Go to the National Portal: Visit the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal. There, you will find an icon stating, “Apply for Rooftop Solar.” Click on it.
  • Step 2 – Set up your account: Choose your state, district, and DISCOM. Then, enter your electricity Customer Account Number to complete the registration.
  • Step 3 – Fill out the solar application form: Add your name and upload a recent electricity bill. Make sure the details match your official connection.
  • Step 4 – Enter your bank details: Provide your bank account number and upload a copy of a cancelled cheque.
  • Step 5 – Choose an approved installer: Pick a DISCOM-empanelled solar company, such as SolarSquare, to install your rooftop system.
  • Step 6 – Upload the signed vendor agreement: Sign the installation agreement with your chosen vendor and upload the scanned copy to the portal.
  • Step 7 – Net meter installation by DISCOM: After system installation, your DISCOM will replace your regular meter with a bi-directional or smart meter.
  • Step 8 – Get your commissioning certificate: Once DISCOM inspects and approves your system, they’ll issue a Solar Commissioning Certificate.
  • Step 9 – Submit your subsidy claim: Log back into your account on the National Portal, attach the commissioning certificate, and file your claim for subsidy.
  • Step 10 – Subsidy credited to your bank account: After final verification, the subsidy amount is transferred directly to your bank account within 30 days.

Types of 8 kW Solar Systems in India and Their Prices

The three primary types of solar systems in India are on-grid, off-grid and hybrid. All three have their own specialities. 

An on-grid solar system is the most affordable, as it does not require lead-acid or lithium batteries to store solar energy since it is connected to the grid. It makes an on-grid solar system perfect for urban areas with a reliable grid connection. However, if your area experiences frequent power cuts, a hybrid or off-grid solar system will be better, as these systems have batteries to provide backup power during outages.
