Which Are The Best Types of Solar Panels For Homes?

Monocrystalline panels, bifacial panels, and mono-PERC half-cut bifacial panels make the best rooftop solar panels in India for housing societies and homes. 

Here are some features that make them better options in residential installations:

  • Subsidy Assistance: If you meet the eligibility criteria, you will get a subsidy for installing the best quality solar panels in India of DCR origin (made in India).
  • Better Warranty on Performance: When they come from top solar panel manufacturers like Navitas Solar and Premier Energies, these panels last for the promised time and perform at their maximum potential, as premium brands source the best materials to manufacture their modules.
  • Made to Last the Rugged Indian Climate: They’re ideal for the hot Indian climate as they perform well even under intense heat.
  • Optimal Efficiency Rates: Monocrystalline panels have an efficiency of 19-20%, bifacial panels have an efficiency up to 22%, and mono-PERC half-cut bifacial panels have an efficiency up to 22.5%. As a result, when maintained well, these panels continue to work at their best potential for decades, ensuring your energy demands are met completely and you remain bill-free.

Now that you know which types of modules you should aim for when it comes to installing the best solar system for home (on-grid rooftop solar), let’s walk you exclusively through the list of top 10 solar panels in India, their specifications, and efficiency.

FAQs

Q1. Which solar panel is best in India for home use?

Ans. Monocrystalline solar panels, bifacial solar panels, and mono-PERC half-cut bifacial solar panels are the best solar panels in India for residential installations, be it homes or housing societies.

Q2. Which brand solar panel is best in India?

Ans. The top brands that produce the most efficient solar panels in India include: Navitas Green Solutions Pvt. Ltd Premier Energies

Q3. How many ACs can run in a 5 kW solar system?

Ans. A 5 kW solar system, capable of generating approximately 7,000 to 7,250 units of electricity annually, can power 2 ACs (1.5-ton each).

Q4. How many kW is required for a home?

Ans. Here’s an estimate based on your home’s annual power consumption: System Capacity Needed Annual Power Consumption 1 kW 1,400-1,450 units 2 kW 2,800-2,950 units 3 kW 4,200-4,350 units 5 kW 7,000-7,250 units 10 kW 14,000-14,500 units

Q5. What is an off-grid solar system?

Ans. An off-grid solar system is the one that is not connected to the utility grid. Instead, it operates with a battery bank, typically composed of lithium batteries. The electricity generated by the solar panels during the day powers appliances and simultaneously charges the battery bank. This stored energy is then used at night. Currently, the government does not offer any subsidy for installing off-grid solar systems. Moreover, the use of lithium batteries significantly increases the overall cost. Therefore, if you live in an urban area with a reliable electricity supply, an on-grid solar system is a more cost-effective and practical option.

Q6. What is the solar panel price in India in 2026?

Ans. The cost of installing on-grid rooftop solar at homes ranges from ~₹ 0.85 lakh in Lucknow (with state + central government subsidy) for a 2 kW solar system to ~₹ 3.05 lakh in Bengaluru for a 5 kW solar system with subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana. *Disclaimer: The above-mentioned solar panel prices in India are indicative as of 06 October 2026 for the SolarSquare Blue 6ft variant. The final cost of solar panel installation depends on your DISCOM charges, product variant opted for, panel type, inverter type, mounting structure height, type of after-sales service, savings guarantee, roof height, etc. Prices are subject to change.

Q7. Can solar panels be used for commercial purposes?

Ans. Yes. Solar panels are widely used for commercial purposes. Commercial rooftop solar systems are commonly installed in factories, warehouses, and office buildings to meet their energy needs. Additionally, large-scale solar farms, comprising thousands of solar panels, are built to generate electricity for distribution to the grid and can power entire communities or cities.
