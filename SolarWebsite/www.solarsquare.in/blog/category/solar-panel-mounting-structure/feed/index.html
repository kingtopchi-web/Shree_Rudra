]]> The past few years have witnessed a surge in the frequency of storm events throughout India. In May 2023, Jaipur experienced a storm with gusty winds that uprooted and damaged over 1000 rooftop solar systems, responsible for significant loss of solar investment and underscored the risks of property damage and life with poor quality solar installations.

Similarly, in May 2024, Mumbai witnessed a massive dust storm where homes and housing societies saw more solar panels flying off their roofs. Additionally, the collapse of a commercial billboard during that storm led to the loss of precious lives. This situation indicates the sudden increase in frequency and intensity of heavy storms and even cyclones rocking our cities. 

The wind speed of the Mumbai storm was just 75 kmph, considered moderate. Despite this, numerous solar systems failed, with panels ripped from rooftops, a massive safety hazard. It also resulted in a complete loss of solar investment for many. 

Such events boost consumer awareness about storm safety in rooftop solar installations and raise questions about the integrity and quality of rooftop solar systems installed in India. Furthermore, who would take responsibility for the costs associated with reinstalling the solar system? These incidents highlighted the serious flaws within India’s unorganised solar market.

Traditional mounting structures, especially those fabricated on-site at your roof are prone to rusting, have poor strength and are likely to collapse during strong winds.

To mitigate this risk, high-quality mounting structures are necessary. These mounts secure the solar panels and optimise their angle to enhance sunlight exposure and protect them from adverse environmental impact.

Concerning your family’s safety at home or the residents in your multi-storey housing society, never overlook the safety standards of your rooftop solar system.

Tips for Choosing the Right Solar Panel Installation

When selecting a solar panel setup for your home, evaluate various factors cautiously. Below is a list of important factors to assist you in making a well-informed choice:

Assess Your Energy Needs

Evaluate your household’s electricity bills from the past year to understand your energy usage and determine the size and capacity of the solar panel system you’ll need. Additionally, consider any potential increases in energy usage, such as buying an electric car or adding new appliances to your home. This will ensure that your solar panel system can meet your current and future energy needs.

Evaluate Your Roof Condition and Orientation

Is your roof in good condition? Is it shadow-free and can it support the weight of solar panels? It’s a good idea to address any existing damage before installation.

Check Local Subsidies and Incentives

The government offers various forms of financial support, such as tax credits, cash rebates, and feed-in tariffs for homeowners who generate excess electricity with their solar panels and sell it back to the grid. They also provide grants and low-interest loans to help install renewable energy systems. Explore potential government incentives, rebates, and tax breaks that could lower installation expenses. 

Calculate the Return on Investment (ROI)

Consider the initial installation cost and long-term electricity bill savings. Estimate the payback period to see how long it will take to recover your investment through energy savings.
