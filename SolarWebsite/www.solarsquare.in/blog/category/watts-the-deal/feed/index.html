What is the Ideal Solar Panel Direction and Angle in India?

Since India is located in the northern hemisphere, south-facing walls, doors, and windows receive the maximum sunlight exposure. Therefore, solar panels should face south to ensure maximum efficiency.

However, sunlight exposure is non-uniform throughout the country. While the general rule is that solar panels should face south, solar panel angle by location varies. 

For example, the optimal solar panel inclination angle for southern states is around 10 degrees, whereas 20 degrees is more suitable in northern states. However, the optimal tilt angle will depend on the site’s latitude and longitudinal location.

Factors That Affect Solar Panel Angle

The angle of your solar panels influences how much sunlight they can capture, directly affecting the energy amount they produce. Here are the key factors that impact solar panel angle:

1. Geographical Location

Since northern states in India are at a higher latitude, solar panels are best tilted at a steeper angle to capture maximum sunlight, especially during winter months. In contrast, southern India, with its lower latitude, requires a flatter tilt to optimise solar gain year-round.

2. Wind Load

In areas prone to high winds, installing panels at a higher angle can increase the risk of damage due to wind load. This risk necessitates careful consideration of local weather patterns and will require a strong mounting structure to ensure stability and longevity.

3. Seasonal Variations

To achieve maximum efficiency year-round, align your tilt angle with the sun’s equatorial position. Any change in orientation can result in an imbalanced output.

4. Shading Issues

Nearby objects like trees, buildings, or other structures can cast shadows on your solar panels, reducing their efficiency. The solar panel angle and direction should be carefully designed to minimise shading issues.

The Effect of Tilt Angle on Solar Panel Efficiency

An increased solar panel tilt angle in northern states will result in higher efficiency and power generation for the entire rooftop solar system, but there’s a caveat. As the tilt angle increases, so does the wind pressure on the panels due to higher wind speeds in those regions.

The solution to this problem is stronger mounting structures. While this may increase the overall installation cost by about 5%, the risk of not having a strong structure that can withstand heavy winds and protect your investment increases by 20%. 

Inadequate structural support can damage, uproot or destroy panels and structures during powerful winds or cyclones. You could end up paying more for repairs and replacements than you save on electricity bills.

Why Does Solar Panel Angle Matter?

The basic science behind solar panels is that solar cells collect energy from photons in sunlight. When panels are placed perpendicular to sunlight, they absorb more solar energy, converting it into electricity. Optimal orientation helps absorb more photons, but performance can still vary with seasonal changes.

Even with the optimal panel angle, some weather conditions beyond anyone’s control can reduce electricity generation. For example, during the monsoon season when the sky is overcast, electricity generation may dip. Similarly, in winter and especially in areas experiencing snowfall, clouds can reduce the amount of sunlight reaching the panels. 

Addressing Shadow Issues on the Rooftop

Imagine installing your rooftop solar panels at the right direction and angle, only to find that trees or other obstructions create shadows on them. Before installing solar panels, your vendor should survey your rooftop to identify any potential shadow areas over a 12-year period and design the system accordingly.

Remember–about 100 square feet of shadow-free space is needed per kilowatt. If you have limited rooftop space and shadow issues, it’s best to consult with a solar expert to determine the ideal solution.

The Real Problem with The Sprinkler System for Solar Panels

The most common forms of filth appearing on solar panels are sticky dust and bird droppings.

The nature of dust in India is different from other countries. The environmental conditions, high humidity and pollutants make it stubborn and sticky. As the dust clings to the surface of solar panels, it resists easy removal. 

Bird droppings contain acidic substances and are much tougher to clean than regular dust. When left unchecked, they can cause significant residue buildup on solar panels.

Traditional cleaning methods fail to remove them from the solar panel surface, and sprinkler systems for solar panel cleaning are even less effective.

The Sprinkler System “Solution” although marketed as a simple and cheap solution, cleans the top layer of dust on solar panels. This superficial cleaning temporarily improves panel appearance but not efficiency. Additionally, considering how most sprinkler systems are designed, the water doesn’t reach the corners and edges of the panels.  

Over time, the filth accumulates and results in uneven soil patches, creating hot spots that pose a fire risk and significantly impact the panels’ ability to absorb irradiance efficiently, ultimately reducing the energy output.

Furthermore, this form of solar panel cleaning is not recommended by manufacturers in any way!

The Consequences of The Sprinkler System for Solar Panels

The automatic sprinkler system for solar panel cleaning leads to various consequences including gradual degradation of the rooftop solar, hotspots, and overall loss of your investment.

While it may seem that a reduced energy generation output is only temporary due to inefficient panel cleaning, the consequences are much more dire. The long-term effect is accelerated degradation of the solar panels.

A study by reputed think-tank CEEW in collaboration with PV Diagnostics revealed that accumulated dust and bird droppings were responsible for 80% of underperforming solar systems. This accelerated degradation shortens the lifespan of your rooftop solar and diminishes the return on investment.

The permanent damage caused by hotspots, such as cracking or burning of the photovoltaic cells, could ignite fires. These damages reduce the panel’s efficiency and create serious safety hazards.

You may also get stuck in the loop of claiming warranties, where you’ll find yourself hopping from one vendor to another, desperately trying to get someone to take responsibility. As the damage escalates, you might find that those once-eager vendors are now pointing fingers elsewhere. 

What’s the best way to ensure thorough solar panel cleaning?

The Best Way to Clean Your Solar Panels

Manufacturers recommend using a soft nylon bristle brush. The soft bristles gently but thoroughly remove surface-level dust and tougher, stickier residues like bird droppings, commonly found on solar panels.

The result?

  1. There are no uneven soil patches. Even if the dust accumulates the next time, it is more evenly spread and does not cause damage to your solar panels. 
  2. The risk of hotspots and fire hazards will be low/non-existent.
  3. You will witness a maximised energy production.
  4. It maintains the integrity of your panels, preventing damage that could void your warranty.
  5. No costly repairs and replacements are required.

This solution protects your investment and extends the lifespan of your solar system, ensuring that it continues to operate efficiently for 25+ years.

However, it’s important to note that this level of solar panel cleaning and maintenance should never be attempted on your own. The process involves specific techniques and equipment and attempting it without proper training can pose serious risks to your safety and even your life. 

To ensure both your safety and the longevity of your solar system, always reach out to trained professionals who are equipped to handle the job safely and effectively.

2. Shadow Effect

50% of the homes surveyed reported this issue. The issue occurs frequently because untrained technicians install solar without seasonal and hourly shadow analysis. 

Rooftop solar systems work on sunshine. Installing them in areas with shadows (even for a few hours of the day) will lead to performance issues and cause fire safety risks. 

3. Shattered Modules

30% of the homes surveyed had one or more solar panels shattered either due to temperature shock caused by hotspots (hotspots on panels are caused by dust, bird droppings and shadows) or mishandling of modules during installation.

4. Scaling/Cementation

15% of the homes surveyed reported this issue. Improper cleaning practices or poor water quality can lead to dust layer deposition, creating mild-moderate hotspots and accelerated degradation, thus reducing performance. 

5. Cable Damage

12% of the homes surveyed reported this issue. Damaged cables from improper wiring structures, reckless activity like general tampering or pulling wires, or improper maintenance can cause high resistance and grave safety concerns. 6. Inoperative/Faulty Inverter

10% of the homes surveyed reported this issue. A non-functioning inverter can lead to a complete system shutdown with no energy output. The primary causes include connection or component failures, or readings outside the safety parameters provided in the inverters.

7. Visual microcracks and Snail trails

Visible trails or markings on PV modules are caused by underlying cell cracks or moisture ingress. These factors can reduce the power output of the module and can further aggravate solar cell performance.

8. Uneven Orientation

5% of homes surveyed reported this issue. Uneven solar panel orientation reduces system performance to the poorest level due to inferior installation practices, low-quality structures, or roof orientation issues.
