Why do Mumbai Solar Systems Need Regular Maintenance?

Mumbai monsoons are no mystery to anybody. Long spells of monsoon rains result in high humidity that, when combined with the salt-laden air from the Arabian Sea, is highly corrosive to even the best solar panels.

  • The extended monsoon factor: For roughly four months, heavy monsoon rain batters your rooftop and leaves behind a mix of grime, algae, and bird droppings once the season passes.
  • The remaining eight months bring a different problem: Salt from the Arabian Sea corrodes metal components, while construction dust and vehicle exhaust settle on your panels every single day.
  • Humidity stays high throughout the year: High levels of humidity trap moisture in places you cannot see. It slowly wears down connections and cell edges.

Mumbai throws a mixture of coastal salt exposure, extreme monsoon cycles, and dense pollution on your panels. That combination is why maintenance here is not optional.

Common Rooftop Solar Issues in Coastal and Monsoon Conditions

Besides year-round high humidity and salty, corrosive air, Mumbai is also an urban city that sees as much pigeon activity as construction, making bird droppings and pollution among the main causes that, if not addressed through periodic solar panel cleaning, can result in lower electricity output.

Here’s a closer look at all the major issues that make frequent panel cleaning and maintenance important:

  • Salt deposits from the Arabian Sea: This salt settles on panel glass, frames, connectors, and mounting hardware. It reacts with moisture to form a mildly acidic solution that corrodes metal much faster than normal water exposure.
  • Post-monsoon grime and mineral residue: Rainwater in polluted urban areas has pollutants and minerals that leave behind stains on the panel surface. After months of Mumbai’s monsoon, panels end up with hardened deposits that regular rain or DIY cleaning on your own cannot wash away.
  • Humidity-related moisture damage: High air moisture levels can affect metallic components, solar connectors, and electrical wiring in solar systems. If the seal around junction boxes or cable entry points has weakened, moisture can get in, causing internal corrosion and resulting in generation drops.
  • Dust and pollution: Construction dust and smoke from vehicles settle on panels daily, forming a layer that blocks sunlight from reaching the panels directly. This reduces the solar radiation absorbed by the panels, in turn, lowering power generation.
  • Bird droppings and hotspot formation: Bird droppings can reduce panel performance and permanently damage affected solar cells. Unlike dust, bird droppings stick to the glass, resist rain, and, under intense sunlight, the acid in them burns into the surface, creating hotspots.
  • Corrosion between metals: When an aluminium panel frame comes into contact with steel mounting hardware, corrosion eats away at the aluminium where the two metals meet. Mumbai’s salty air and humidity make this form of corrosion very aggressive and hard to spot early.

How Often Should Solar Panels Be Cleaned?

How often you need to clean your panels depends on how polluted the air is where you live. Cities with heavy construction, industrial zones, and dense traffic produce more airborne dust, which means panels get dirtier faster.

  • Highly polluted cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata): Panels must be cleaned professionally twice a month.
  • Cities with moderate pollution (Pune, Ahmedabad): Panels must be cleaned once a month as dust still accumulates, but at a slower rate.
  • Cities with low pollution levels (smaller towns, hill stations, rural areas): Panels in such areas must be cleaned once every 45 days or once every month.

Mumbai comes in the first category of heavily polluted cities. Between the salt air, pollution, and construction dust that cover the city, twice-a-month cleaning is necessary to keep your PV system performing at its maximum potential.

How Do I Know if My Solar Panels Need Cleaning or Repairing?

If your PV system’s output has dropped, the first question is whether you need a cleaning or a repair. And, the answer depends on what is causing the drop.

  • When do you need cleaning? Cleaning can do the trick if the problem is on the surface. Dust, bird droppings, grime, and pollution residue sitting on your panels block direct solar energy from reaching the panel, reducing output. A proper cleaning removes all of that and restores the generation.
  • When do you need repair? Repair is needed when the problem is internal or structural. Hotspots inside cells, cracked modules, inverter faults, loose wiring, damaged earthing, or corroded connectors will not go away with cleaning. These need a trained technician with diagnostic equipment.

Solar Panel Cleaning vs Solar Panel Repair: What’s the Difference?

Solar panel cleaning is the process of removing surface-level dirt, dust, bird droppings, grime, and pollution residue from your panels. The goal here is to restore the panel’s ability to fully absorb sunlight. Cleaning deals with what is sitting on top of the panel and blocking light from reaching the photovoltaic cells.

Solar panel repair, on the other hand, is the process of diagnosing and fixing internal or structural faults in your solar system. This includes problems such as hotspots within cells, cracked or damaged modules, inverter failures, loose or degraded wiring, and faulty earthing. These are issues that no amount of cleaning can resolve.

The way to tell the two apart is actually very simple.

  • If your panels look dirty and output has dipped slightly, you likely need cleaning.
  • If your panels are clean but output has still dropped significantly, or your inverter is throwing error codes, you most likely need a repair inspection.

Things to Keep in Mind When Choosing Solar Repair Services Near Me in Mumbai

You could search every correct query ranging from best solar panel repair near me to best solar panel maintenance near me and best solar system maintenance near me, and still, you can’t be sure that the company you’ll find will offer the right diagnostics and solutions at fair prices until and unless you know what to look for.

Not every company that offers solar repair services will do a good job. And picking the wrong one can make things worse, void your warranty, or leave you paying for a problem that was never properly fixed.

So, here’s what you must check before you hire anyone.

  • Ask whether their technicians are trained: Repairs performed by untrained technicians can permanently void your panel and solar inverter warranties. Ensure the company uses trained solar professionals who follow the manufacturer’s guidelines for every repair.
  • Check if they provide documentation: A reliable service provider will give you a written report of what they found, what they fixed, and what still needs attention. If a company does the repair and walks away without any paperwork, you have no proof of the work for future warranty claims.
  • Confirm they carry their own safety equipment: Rooftop solar repair involves high-voltage DC systems. The technicians should arrive with proper insulation gear, harnesses, and electrical gloves. If they show up without safety equipment, do not let them on your roof.
  • Ask about their diagnostic capability: A company that only does visual inspections is limited in what it can catch. Look for providers that use thermal imaging and performance diagnostics to identify hidden faults, such as hotspots, cell degradation, and wiring issues that are invisible to the naked eye.
  • Be cautious of flat repair charges before inspection: No honest service provider can tell you the final repair cost without first inspecting your system. If someone gives you a firm repair price over the phone before finding out what the problem is, that is a red flag.

FAQs

How much does it cost to clean one solar panel?

While it can cost upwards of Rs. 50 to clean one solar panel; panel-level cleaning services are not recommended. These services will only remove visible dirt, leaving structural and electrical problems with panels, connectors, inverters, and mounting structures. That’s why you should opt for cleaning and maintenance services that offer inspection as a part of the package.

What is the best time to clean solar panels?

Early morning or late evening, when the panels are cool, are the best two time slots to clean solar panels. Cleaning panels during peak sun hours can cause water to evaporate too quickly, leaving behind mineral deposits. It can also cause thermal shock, where cold water hitting hot glass can lead to microcracks in the panel surface.

How much water is required to clean solar panels?

You might need 3 to 7 litres of water per panel when using water alone to clean the modules. Professional cleaning services with specialized equipment can bring this down significantly.

Is tap water ok to clean solar panels?

Using tap water to clean solar panels is usually not recommended because tap water with high mineral content leaves behind limescale and white residue when it dries, which blocks sunlight the same way dust does. Therefore, RO or distilled water is used to properly clean solar panels.

Can dirty solar panels cause problems?

Dirty panels can lose up to 40% of their output if not cleaned for a year. In such a long span, issues such as bird droppings can create hotspots that can permanently damage cells. Over months, the financial loss from reduced generation adds up far beyond the cost of a cleaning visit.

Can I pressure wash solar panels?

Pressure washing solar panels is not recommended at all. High-pressure water can force its way through the seals around panel frames and reach the sensitive electrical components inside. It can also crack the tempered glass and damage the anti-reflective coating.
