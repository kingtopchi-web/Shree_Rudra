Installing rooftop solar in Delhi in 2026 is a smart decision, as Delhi homeowners can benefit from not one but two subsidy schemes:

  • The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana government financing for solar panels: This is the central government’s solar subsidy in Delhi, capped at ₹ 78,000 for rooftop solar systems of 3 kWp or more.
  • The state-level solar panel Delhi government subsidy: This is the state solar system subsidy in Delhi offered by the Delhi government, capped at ₹ 78,000 for systems of 3 kWp or more.

If you install a rooftop solar system in Delhi in 2026, you can benefit from a whopping cumulative subsidy of ₹ 156,000 for systems equal to or above 3 kW. And the Indian government will offer the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana solar subsidy scheme only until it solarizes 1 crore Indian homes.

Read on to explore the total solar subsidy in Delhi in 2026 for different capacity solar systems, steps to apply for a solar subsidy, eligibility conditions, documents required to receive a solar subsidy in Delhi straight into your bank account, and the solar panel price in Delhi with subsidy in 2026.

How Much is the Solar Subsidy in Delhi in 2026?

The maximum total solar subsidy in Delhi is ₹ 156,000 for systems of 3 kWp or more. However, this comes from two separate subsidies. One is offered by the central government of India, capped at ₹ 78,000 for solar systems with a capacity of 3 kW or higher. And the other is the Delhi government subsidy on solar panels, capped at ₹ 78,000 for PV systems with a capacity of 3 kW or higher.

How to Get a Subsidy on Solar Panels in Delhi? Step-by-Step Breakdown

Homeowners can follow the steps listed below to apply for and receive the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana solar subsidy in Delhi:

  • Step 1 – Open the National portal for rooftop solar. Go to the official PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana portal and select the option for rooftop solar application.
  • Step 2 – Create your profile: Choose your state, district, and DISCOM from the dropdown. Then, create your account using your electricity consumer (Customer Account) number.
  • Step 3 – Complete the application form: Enter your personal details such as name and age, and provide information from your latest electricity bill.
  • Step 4 – Add banking information: Upload the required documents, enter your bank account information, and submit a cancelled cheque.
  • Step 5 – Choose an empanelled installer: Select a vendor from the list of DISCOM-authorized (empanelled) installers available on the portal.
  • Step 6 – Upload the signed agreement: Carefully review the installer agreement, sign it, and upload the signed copy to the portal.
  • Step 7 – Net meter is installed: After the solar system is set up, the DISCOM installs a bi-directional net meter or smart meter to track both imported and exported electricity.
  • Step 8 – Get the commissioning certificate: After inspection, the DISCOM issues the Solar Commissioning Certificate.
  • Step 9 – Submit the subsidy request: Log back into the portal and upload the commissioning certificate to initiate your subsidy claim.
  • Step 10 – Receive the subsidy: After verification and approval, the subsidy amount is transferred directly to your registered bank account.

Please note (IMPORTANT): You must file a separate application on the Delhi Solar Portal to receive the state-level Delhi government solar energy subsidy of up to ₹ 78,000 for systems of 3 kWp or more.

Who is Eligible for the PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Scheme in Delhi?

Indian citizens who fulfil the following conditions are eligible for the PM Surya Ghar solar subsidy in Delhi:

  • You must choose made-in-India, ALMM-approved solar panels.
  • You must choose a grid-tied solar system.
  • You must have an electricity connection in your name with your local DISCOM.
  • You must be the legal owner of the roof where the solar system will be installed.
  • You have never received a subsidy for systems up to 3 kW before.

Documents Required to Apply for Solar Energy Subsidy in Delhi

If you meet the aforementioned conditions, you need a few documents, listed below, to apply for the solar system subsidy in Delhi:

  • Basic docs for solar net metering (solar system details/plant layout)
  • Copy of your latest electricity bill
  • Net agreement between you and your discom

How Long Does it Take to Receive the Solar Panel Subsidy in Delhi? A Tentative Timeline

Delhi has three main electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs), i.e., BSES Rajdhani Power Limited (BRPL), BSES Yamuna Power Limited (BYPL), and Tata Power Delhi Distribution Limited (TPDDL).

Each DISCOM serves a different part of the city and manages its own approvals, inspections, and net metering process. Because of this, the timeline for subsidy processing and required documentation can vary based on your location and DISCOM.

Here’s a tentative timeline based on our personal experience:

  • Central subsidy timeline: 30 days after plant commissioning
  • State subsidy timeline: 3 months

FAQs

What is the government’s free solar panel scheme?

It’s the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana solar subsidy scheme offered by the central government. The maximum subsidy under this scheme is capped at ₹ 78,000 for systems of 3 kWp or more.

How much area is required for rooftop solar installation in Delhi?

You will require 80-100 sq. ft. of shadow-free area on your roof for every 1 kW of a solar system. For example, for a 5 kW solar system, you’ll need 500 square feet of shadow-free area on your roof, and so on.

How many ACs can run on a 3 kW solar system in Delhi?

A 3 kW solar system in Delhi can power one 1.5-ton AC and is a recommended system size for a 2-3 BHK home.

Is net metering available in Delhi?

Yes, net metering is available in Delhi. It is governed by the Delhi Electricity Regulatory Commission (DERC) under its net metering regulations and is supported under the Delhi Solar Policy and PM Surya Ghar scheme.
