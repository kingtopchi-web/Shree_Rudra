The Indian government has a target of installing 30 GW of rooftop solar by 2026-27. This supports India’s green climate commitments under its Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) to the UNFCCC. To achieve this target, the government has been promoting rooftop solar installations through multiple government solar panel schemes that offer subsidies.

Here are some of the most popular government solar panel schemes in 2026 in India:

  • The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana solar panel system government scheme for homes: Under this government solar panel scheme, homeowners receive a subsidy for installing rooftop solar at their homes. It is capped at ₹ 78,000 in non-hilly areas and at ₹ 85,800 in hilly areas for systems with a capacity of 3 kW or more.
  • The PM KUSUM Yojana Indian Government Solar Panel Scheme for solar water pumps used by farmers: This is the financial assistance offered by the Indian government to farmers who install solar water pumps and PV systems for agricultural purposes.
  • New Solar Power Scheme for PVTG Habitations/Villages Under PM Janman: This is a government scheme that aims at bringing power to un-electrified households in Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG) areas across 18 states using off-grid solar systems. It has a budget of ₹ 515 crore to cover one lakh households.

There are multiple state-level schemes as well, such as the UP government solar panel scheme and the Assam state solar subsidy.

While there are multiple government free solar panel schemes in India in 2026, they all share the common goal of making solar renewable energy the mainstream power source in India and reducing reliance on imported oil and gas.

Read on to explore the details of all such government solar panel schemes that are currently being offered in India in 2026 and how they can benefit you.

Is There a Free Solar Panel Scheme by the Government of India?

The most popular free solar panel scheme by the government of India in 2026 is the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, which offers a solar subsidy for installing residential rooftop grid-connected solar systems.

This PM Surya Ghar government financing for solar panels will be offered to homeowners only until the government has solarized 1 crore homes under this scheme by March 31 2027.

The biggest goals of the PM Surya Ghar rooftop solar panels government scheme are:

  • Generating 1,000 billion electricity units through the capacity installed under the scheme.
  • Offsetting 720 million tons of CO2 emissions during the 25-year lifespan of solar systems.

What are the Different Government Solar Panel Schemes in India in 2026?

The 3 most popular Indian government free solar panel schemes that are being provided in 2026 at the National level include the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana for residential rooftop solar installation, the PM KUSUM Yojana Indian government solar panel scheme for farmers, and the New Solar Power Scheme for PVTG Habitations/Villages Under PM Janman.

Let’s explore the details of all these government solar panel schemes in detail.

#1. PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Solar Panel Installation Government Scheme for Homes

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is a free solar panel scheme by the government of India that’s offered to homeowners and housing societies installing rooftop on-grid solar systems.

It’s offered to popularize rooftop solar adoption at homes and help the government meet its target of solarizing 1 crore homes under this scheme by March 31 2027.

  • In non-hilly states: The PM Surya Ghar solar panel installation scheme is capped at ₹ 78,000 for homeowners installing rooftop solar systems of 3 kW or higher capacity. For housing societies, it is capped at ₹ 90 lakh for solar systems of 500 kW or more.
  • In hilly areas such as Uttarakhand and Assam: The PM Surya Ghar government solar panel scheme is capped at ₹ 85,800 for systems equal to or above 3 kWp. It’s 10% higher in hilly areas than in non-hilly regions in India.

Based on the official PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal, here’s the tiered subsidy offered under this scheme:

For additional 1 kWp or thereafter ₹ 18,000 per kWp ₹ 19,800 per kWp

The PM Surya Ghar government free solar panel scheme is also applicable for housing societies installing grid-connected rooftop solar systems.

  • Under this scheme, the maximum subsidy for housing societies is capped at ₹ 90 lakh for systems of 500 kW or more.
  • Group housing societies (GHS) in India receive a subsidy of ₹ 18,000 per kW (up to 500 kW, @3 kW per house) for common facilities, including EV charging.

#2. PM Kusum Yojana Indian Government Solar Panel Scheme for Farmers

The PM KUSUM Yojana (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan) is the Indian government solar panel scheme for farmers that supports the installation of solar agriculture pumps and grid-connected solar power plants.

It’s offered by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) to individual farmers, groups of farmers, cooperatives, panchayats, Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), and Water User Associations (WUAs).

Under this Indian government free solar panel scheme, farmers receive a 30-50% subsidy on solar pump installation.

Here are the 3 components offered under the PM KUSUM government solar panel scheme for farmers in 2026:

  • Component A (Solar Power Plants on Farmland): Farmers can install grid-tied solar power plants of 500 kW to 2 MW on their barren or unused farmland and sell the power to DISCOMs.
  • Component B (Standalone Solar Pumps for Off-Grid Farms): Farmers in off-grid areas can install standalone solar agriculture pumps of up to 7.5 HP to replace their diesel pumps.
  • Component C (Solarization of Existing Grid-Connected Pumps): Farmers with existing grid-connected pumps can solarize them, use solar power for irrigation during the day, and sell excess power to the DISCOM for additional income.

#3. New Solar Power Scheme for PVTG Habitations/Villages Under PM Janman

The New Solar Power Scheme under PM JANMAN (Pradhan Mantri Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan) is the solar panel scheme by the Indian government that electrifies un-electrified tribal households through off-grid solar systems.

It’s offered to Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs) living in remote areas where connecting to the main power grid is not feasible.

Here are the main motives of this Indian government free solar panel scheme:

  • Supplying electricity to 1 lakh un-electrified tribal households and 1,500 Multi-Purpose Centers (MPCs) across 18 states and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
  • Providing power within the budget of ₹ 515 crore.

Here are the 3 components offered under the PM JANMAN government solar panel scheme in 2026:

  • Solar Home Lighting System (SHLS): Scattered un-electrified households in PVTG areas receive a free Solar Home Lighting System along with necessary appliances such as LED bulbs and a fan.
  • Solar Mini-Grids: For a cluster of households in a PVTG habitation, the scheme installs a Solar Mini-Grid of appropriate capacity along with a battery bank, distribution lines, metering, and control equipment.
  • Solarization of Multi-Purpose Centers (MPCs): For identified MPCs where main grid electricity is not available, the scheme installs an off-grid solar power pack with a battery bank.

Special State-Level Government Solar Panel Schemes in 2026

In most Indian states, PM Surya Ghar is the only government scheme for solar panels for homes. However, a few state governments, such as Delhi, Assam, Chhattisgarh, and Haryana, offer an additional subsidy to homeowners on top of the PM Surya Ghar financing for solar panels.

Let’s take a look at the state-level government solar panel schemes in 2026.

#1. Solar Panel Government Scheme in UP

The UP government solar panel scheme caps the subsidy at ₹ 30,000 for rooftop solar systems of 2 kWp or more.

Here’s the snapshot of the subsidy homeowners can receive under the state government solar panel scheme in UP:

10 kWp ₹ 30,000

Please note: The state subsidy offered by the UP government is ₹ 15,000 per kWp, capped at ₹ 30,000 for systems of 2 kW or more.

Simply put, there are two UP government schemes for solar panels:

  • The PM Surya Ghar government solar panel scheme, capped at ₹ 78,000.
  • The UP government free solar panel scheme, capped at ₹ 30,000.

So, the maximum government financing for solar panels in UP is capped at ₹ 108,000.

#2. Delhi Government Scheme for Installing Solar Panels

The state-level solar panel system government scheme in Delhi offers a subsidy of ₹ 26,000 per kW, capped at ₹ 78,000 for systems of 3 kWp or more. And this state subsidy is on top of the PM Surya Ghar solar panel scheme by the Indian government.

The Haryana state government offers a State Financial Assistance (SFA) for low-income families, in addition to the PM Surya Ghar solar panel scheme by the Indian government.

This state-level solar subsidy is capped at ₹ 50,000 for Category 1 households and ₹ 20,000 for Category 2 households for rooftop solar systems with a capacity of up to 2 kW.

How to Apply for the PM Surya Ghar Government Solar Panel Scheme in India?

Homeowners can apply for the PM Surya Ghar government solar panel scheme by registering on the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal.

You can follow these steps to receive the subsidy straight into your bank account:

  • Step 1 – Access the solar portal: Open the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana website and click the rooftop solar application section.
  • Step 2 – Register yourself: Select your state, district, and DISCOM from the available dropdowns, then create an account using the Customer Account number on your electricity bill.
  • Step 3 – Fill in your details: Share your name and age, and enter the relevant numbers from your most recent electricity bill.
  • Step 4 – Add your banking details: Upload the required supporting documents, provide your bank account information, and attach a copy of a cancelled cheque.
  • Step 5 – Finalize an empanelled vendor: Review the list of DISCOM-approved installers on the portal and lock in the vendor you want to hire.
  • Step 6 – Review, sign, and upload the agreement: Go through the installer agreement, sign it, and upload the signed version to the portal.
  • Step 7 – DISCOM installs the net meter: After your solar system is installed, DISCOM installs a bi-directional net meter or smart meter.
  • Step 8 – Collect your commissioning certificate: The DISCOM runs an inspection of the solar system and then issues the Solar Commissioning Certificate.
  • Step 9 – File for the subsidy: Return to the portal, log in, and upload the solar commissioning certificate to file the subsidy claim.
  • Step 10 – Subsidy gets credited to your account: Once verification is completed, the subsidy is deposited straight into your registered bank account.

Who is Eligible for the PM Surya Ghar Indian Government Solar Panel Scheme?

If you are an Indian citizen, you can become eligible for the PM Surya Ghar Indian government solar panel scheme if you meet the following conditions:

  • You install made-in-India, ALMM-approved solar panels.
  • You have an electricity connection in your name with your local DISCOM.
  • You have ownership of the roof where the system will be installed.
  • You have never received a subsidy for systems up to 3 kW before.

How to Apply for Specific State Solar Subsidy Schemes?

In Assam and UP, consumers don’t have to file a separate state subsidy application.

  • For Assam: In Assam, APDCL (Assam Power Distribution Company Limited) administers the state solar subsidy, along with AEDA (Assam Energy Development Agency), and customers don’t need to submit a separate application. After the net meter is installed and the central PM Surya Ghar subsidy is released, the Assam state government automatically pays the additional subsidy directly to the customer’s bank account, with no extra paperwork or application needed from your side.
  • For UP: In Uttar Pradesh, UPNEDA (Uttar Pradesh New and Renewable Energy Development Agency) administers the state solar subsidy, and customers don’t need to submit a separate application. After the net meter is installed, UPNEDA automatically reviews the case through the National Portal. As long as the details and documents are correct, the state subsidy is approved automatically without the customer having to do anything extra.

For Delhi State Subsidy

Delhi residents can apply for the state-level solar subsidy by registering on the Delhi Solar Rooftop Portal, which works alongside the central PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy.

You can follow these steps to receive the Delhi solar subsidy straight into your bank account:

  • Step 1 – Register on the Delhi solar portal: Visit the Delhi Rooftop Solar Portal and register using your Customer Account (CA) number, mobile number, and email ID. Verify the OTP to complete registration.
  • Step 2 – Apply for the subsidy: Log in and apply for the state subsidy. Most details get auto-fetched from your existing PM Surya Ghar application. You only need to fill in your bank details and upload a cancelled cheque. Once you submit, an application number is generated.
  • Step 3 – DISCOM verification: Your DISCOM checks the solar installation, net meter, and uploaded documents to confirm everything is in order.
  • Step 4 – Subsidy gets credited to your account: Once verification and approval are completed, the state subsidy amount is deposited straight into your registered bank account.

Please note: The full process from application to subsidy credit usually takes around 3 to 4 months. To check the status of your subsidy, go to the portal home page, click “Check Capital Subsidy Released Status,” and enter your CA number.

FAQs

Is there a government scheme for solar panels?

Yes, PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is the government subsidy scheme that helps homeowners install solar at cheaper rates.

Is there an additional state subsidy in Delhi?

Yes. The Delhi state government offers an additional subsidy on top of the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. It is capped at ₹ 78,000 for systems of 3 kWp or more. To receive your Delhi state subsidy, you must ensure that your CA number and bank details are entered correctly, the name on your application matches the name on your electricity record, the net meter is installed before you apply for the subsidy, and all uploaded documents are clear and readable.

If your electricity bill is in joint names, you will need to upload a cancelled cheque from a joint bank account.

Is there a special state-level solar panel government scheme in Maharashtra for homes?

No, there’s no special state-level solar panel government scheme in Maharashtra for homes. However, all homeowners can still benefit from the PM Surya Ghar government solar panel scheme.

What is the Gujarat government solar panel scheme for homes?

There’s no special state-level Gujarat government solar panel scheme for homes. There’s only the central government’s PM Surya Ghar financing for solar panels in Gujarat.

Is there any special Punjab government solar panel scheme for homes?

No, there’s no state subsidy offered by the Punjab government. However, homeowners can install solar at subsidized rates, thanks to the PM Surya Ghar central government solar panel scheme.

How much is the solar subsidy in UP for a 3 kW solar system?

The total solar subsidy in UP for systems equal to or above 3 kW is ₹ 108,000. Out of this, ₹ 78,000 come from the central government’s PM Surya Ghar scheme and the remaining ₹ 30,000 come from the state subsidy scheme.

What is the subsidy for solar panels in Assam?

The maximum state-level solar subsidy in Assam is capped at ₹ 45,000 for systems equal to and above 3 kWp. When combined with the ₹ 85,800 PM Surya Ghar subsidy for systems of 3 kW or above, the maximum subsidy in Assam that homeowners can receive is capped at ₹ 1,30,800.

Is there any subsidy on solar panels in Uttarakhand?

Yes, the PM Surya Ghar subsidy scheme is the financial assistance available in Uttarakhand to homeowners. Since Uttarakhand is a hilly area, the central government offers a 10% higher subsidy here. So, the maximum subsidy in Uttarakhand is capped at ₹ 85,800 for systems equal to or above 3 kWp.

How to apply for solar subsidy in Haryana?

Homeowners need to apply for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy in Haryana through the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana National Portal only.

What is the subsidy for a 3 kW solar system in Chhattisgarh?

The total subsidy on a 3 kW solar system in Chhattisgarh is ₹ 1.08 lakh. Out of this, ₹ 78,000 are from the PM Surya Ghar scheme and ₹ 30,000 are from the state government.
