What is PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

Government of India's flagship rooftop solar scheme

Launched in February 2024 to make clean energy affordable for homeowners. Under this scheme, eligible households can receive up to ₹78,000 as a direct subsidy for installing rooftop solar systems. This will reduce dependence on the grid and lower monthly power bills for consumers.

The subsidy is transferred directly to your bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) after successful installation and commissioning.

Eligibility & Documents

Eligibility & documents

  • You should be an Indian resident.
  • The property should be residential (commercial and industrial properties aren't eligible).
  • You should have the property ownership and roof rights.
  • You should have an active DISCOM electricity connection.
  • You must have at least 80 sq. ft. of shadow-free rooftop space for a 1 kW solar system.

How to Apply for PM Surya Ghar Subsidy?

  1. 01 Register on the National Portal Register on the PM Surya Ghar portal using your consumer number and Aadhaar OTP.
  2. 02 Apply for Rooftop Solar Submit your rooftop details and preferred system size to complete your application.
  3. 03 Choose an MNRE-Registered Vendor Select an MNRE-approved installer like SolarSquare for a site survey.
  4. 04 Get Your System Installed Your approved vendor installs the solar system using DCR panels and certified components.
  5. 05 DISCOM Inspection & Net Meter DISCOM inspects the system and installs a net meter.
  6. 06 Submit Commissioning Certificate Upload the commissioning certificate on the National Portal to initiate the subsidy process.
  7. 07 Subsidy Credited to Your Bank Receive the subsidy directly in your bank account, typically within 30–45 days after commissioning.

Track your application

After logging in to pmsuryaghar.gov.in, you can track your application status at every stage, from registration and approval to installation and subsidy credit.

Things to Keep in Mind While Going Solar

Everything you should verify before investing in a rooftop solar system.

  • Check Your Electricity Bill

    See how many units you consume monthly – 1 kW solar generates ~1,300–1,400 units/year.

  • Check Your Sanctioned Load

    Solar capacity can't exceed your sanctioned load, but it can be enhanced if needed.

  • Check shadow-free space available on Roof

    1 kW needs ~80 sq ft of shadow-free space

  • Verify Component Quality

    Check the brand & technology of the solar panels, inverter, structure, DC cables, etc.

  • Assess Structure & Installation

    Look for a cyclone-proof, rust-proof, long-lasting module mounting structure, chemical anchoring, and proper waterproofing.
