Calculate Your Solar Savings with the SolarSquare Solar Calculator

Curious how much you can save by switching to solar? The SolarSquare Solar Calculator is your go-to online tool to estimate your rooftop solar potential, system size, tentative installation cost, and long-term savings — all in just a few clicks.

Our solar rooftop calculator helps you discover the savings, payback and ROI that you will get if you install solar on your roof. Designed for Indian power consumers, the calculator considers your location (pincode), monthly electricity bill to generate instant and accurate results.

Why Use the SolarSquare Solar Panel Calculator?

Our intelligent solar panel calculator for home is powered by region-specific data and takes into account electricity tariffs, panel degradation, inflation and solar irradiance levels to give you a personalised solar savings report.

 Estimate Solar Savings: Know how much you can save each month and over 25 years.
 Size Your Solar System: Get the ideal system capacity for your rooftop.
 Calculate Investment & ROI: Understand your return on investment and break-even point.
 Area Requirement: Learn how much rooftop space you’ll need.
 System Performance: See how much clean energy your system will generate (equivalent to how many trees planted)
 Compare Financing Options: Evaluate savings and how you can get solar @ 0 investment.

Our solar system calculator for home users also factors in net metering, ensuring you see the full picture of solar benefits in your state. And yes, we’ve mapped every pincode in India, so no matter where you live, this tool works for you.

How It Works

Using the solar calculator is simple:

  • Enter your pin code.
  • Fill in your average electricity bill.

In seconds, our solar panel price calculator shows you the required system size, required shadow free space on the roof, monthly and yearly savings, estimated cost of installation, subsidy that you will get, power generation, lifetime savings and Return on investment. The calculator also helps you understand your downpayment, EMI, tenure in case you opt for loan or a financing option. The calculator also helps you understand the impact that you will bring in terms of CO2 mitigation or equivalency of trees planted or distance travelled.
