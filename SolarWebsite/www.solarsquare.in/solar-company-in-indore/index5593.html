    How Does Rooftop Solar Work?

    Once your rooftop solar system is commissioned, the process of powering your home begins. Here’s how it all works:

    • Panels Capture Sunlight and Generate Power: Solar cells embedded in the panels convert sunlight into direct current (DC) electricity during the day.
    • DC Gets Converted to Home-Ready AC Power: A solar inverter steps in to change DC into alternating current (AC), the kind home appliances run on.
    • Smart Energy Monitoring via Net Metering: A special bi-directional meter keeps tabs on how much power your system exports to and imports from the grid. If you generate more than you use, you build up credit. If your home draws more power than your panels provide, you just pay for the shortfall.

    On-Grid vs Off-Grid Solar Systems: Which is Better and Why?

    On-grid rooftop solar energy systems feed electricity straight into the utility grid and draw power back whenever production dips through net metering. Off-grid solar systems, on the other hand, store the electricity generated by solar panels in a lead-acid or lithium battery bank. 

    The one that’s better for you depends on whether or not you need a battery backup.

    • When should you invest in an on-grid solar system in Indore? If your grid supply is reliable and power cuts are rare, battery backup is not necessary. In such cases, on-grid systems are better as they’re cheaper and offer the best ROI.
    • When should you invest in an off-grid solar system in Indore? If the grid supply is unavailable or unreliable, you will need a battery bank to provide backup power at night or during low-sunlight hours. In these cases, an off-grid solar system is a better investment.

    How do they work day and night?Solar panels produce electricity during the daytime. Any excess power not used during the day goes to the grid. At night or when solar production is low, electricity is drawn straight from the grid.The electricity produced by the panels during the day is used in two ways:
    • It keeps the load running.
    • Excess energy is used to charge lead-acid or lithium batteries. 

    The energy stored in the battery bank powers the home at night or wherever needed.
