How Does Rooftop Solar Work?

An on-grid rooftop solar system in Trichy works the same way via the photovoltaic effect. The solar panel converts sunlight into DC power, which is converted to AC through a solar inverter. The working of a rooftop solar system is given below:

  • Solar panels absorb sunlight: The photons present in the sunlight energise the electrons inside the solar cell, causing them to move. The flow of electrons generates DC electricity.
  • DC-to-AC conversion: We cannot run the appliances on DC, so a solar inverter takes that raw DC power from the panels and converts it into AC.
  • Net metering process: The DISCOM installs a net meter (bi-directional meter) to track the energy exchange between the grid and your solar system. When your solar system sends more electricity than it imports, the DISCOM gives you credit. On the other hand, when your home draws more electricity from the grid than what your system imports, you pay the DISCOM for additional grid units.

On-Grid vs Off-Grid Solar System in Trichy: Which is better for Homes?

If you’re going solar in Trichy, you can choose between on-grid and off-grid options.

On-grid systems, as the name suggests, are connected to the grid. This works well if your area has a fairly stable power supply and outages are rare. During the day, your panels power your home, and any extra electricity flows straight into the grid. At night, once the panels slow down the power generation rate, your home draws power from the grid.

On the other hand, off-grid systems rely on batteries, rather than the grid. Any surplus power your panels generate throughout the day gets stored in these batteries. The stored power can be used during nighttime and during power outages. The side-by-side comparison between the two types helps you choose the right system based on your needs:

FeatureOn-Grid Solar System in TrichyOff-Grid Solar System in Trichy
Are they connected to the grid?YesNo
Do they need a lead-acid or lithium battery bank?NoYes
Are they eligible for the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana solar subsidy?YesNo
How do they work?In on-grid systems, solar panels are connected to the grid and have no power backup. That power comes out as DC, so an inverter converts it to AC, which is used by appliances. The excess power not used in real time goes to the grid. At night, the system draws power from the grid.In an off-grid system, during the day, the electricity from your panels powers your home in real time. Anything left over doesn’t go to the grid. The excess energy charges the batteries that power the load at night and can even be used during power outages.
When are they most recommended?Ideal for rooftop solar systems for housing societies, homes, and commercial buildings in areas with rare power outages and a very reliable grid.Ideal for residential and commercial rooftop systems in areas with unreliable or unavailable grid power.
